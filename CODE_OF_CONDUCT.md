# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want SmartCore to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful open-source project. Examples of positive participation include:

- Giving constructive, specific feedback.
- Respecting different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Focusing discussion on the project's technical goals.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and correcting mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sharing credentials and private user data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/aaserag1). Do not include sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
